--- KEY INSIGHTS ---
• LOS: Power concentrated in first tap (direct path)
• NLOS: Power spread across many taps (rich scattering)
• NLOS: Higher delay spread → frequency-selective fading
• NLOS: More multipath components
• NLOS: Higher path loss (obstacles block signal)

--- ML RELEVANCE ---
LOS/NLOS classification is important for positioning.
Features for ML:
  - Dominant tap power ratio
  - RMS delay spread
  - Number of significant taps
  - Power distribution (concentrated vs spread)
  - Path loss
