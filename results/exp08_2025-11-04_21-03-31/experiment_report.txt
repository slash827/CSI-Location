KEY INSIGHTS
============

1. Linear Trajectory:
   - Monotonic distance change
   - Smooth RSS variation
   - Predictable pattern
   - Good for testing algorithms

2. Circular Trajectory:
   - Constant distance to BS
   - More stable RSS
   - Lower variability
   - Tests angular effects

3. Random Walk:
   - Unpredictable movement
   - Higher variability
   - More realistic scenario
   - Challenging for prediction

4. For ML Training:
   - Use diverse trajectory types
   - Random walks most realistic
   - Linear/circular for validation
   - Balance dataset across patterns
