--- PATH LOSS ANALYSIS ---

   25 m → 75.71 dB
   50 m → 75.72 dB

Path Loss Increase (doubling distance):
  25m to 50m: 0.01 dB
  50m to 100m: 8.72 dB

Theory: Doubling distance → ~6 dB increase (free space)

--- KEY INSIGHTS ---
• CSI magnitude decreases with distance
• Path loss increases logarithmically (~6dB per distance doubling)
• Number of taps may vary with distance
• First tap usually dominant in LOS scenarios
• Position affects received power significantly

--- ML RELEVANCE ---
Distance estimation from CSI is a key ML task.
Features for ML:
  - Total received power (path loss)
  - Dominant tap magnitude
  - Number of significant taps
  - Power distribution across taps
