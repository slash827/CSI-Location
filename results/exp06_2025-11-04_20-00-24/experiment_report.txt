CONFIGURATION
=============

Base Configuration:
  Frequency: 3.50 GHz
  Scenario: 3GPP_38.901_UMa_LOS
  Distance: 80 m
  Subcarriers: 256

PART 1: Bandwidth Effects
==========================

Tested bandwidths: 20, 50, 100 MHz
Observation: Wider BW → More frequency selectivity

PART 2: Transmit Power Effects
==============================

Tested powers: -10, 0, +10, +20 dBm per SC
Result: Linear RSS increase with Tx power
CQI saturates at 15 (maximum quality)

PART 3: Noise Figure Effects
============================

Tested NF: 3, 7, 10, 15 dB
Result: Higher NF → Lower SINR and CQI
Typical UE: 7-9 dB, High-quality: 3-5 dB

PART 4: Scenario Comparison
===========================

Scenarios tested:
  1. Ideal: 100MHz, +20dBm, 3dB NF
  2. Typical: 100MHz, 0dBm, 7dB NF
  3. Challenging: 20MHz, -10dBm, 10dB NF
  4. Extreme: 20MHz, -20dBm, 15dB NF

KEY INSIGHTS
============

1. BANDWIDTH:
   - Wider → More frequency selectivity
   - Affects subcarrier spacing
   - More throughput potential

2. TRANSMIT POWER:
   - Higher → Better SINR and CQI
   - Battery consumption tradeoff
   - Regulatory limits apply

3. NOISE FIGURE:
   - Lower → Better SINR
   - Hardware quality matters
   - Typical: 7-9 dB

4. FOR ML PROJECT:
   - Use consistent parameters
   - Recommended: 100 MHz, 0 dBm, 7 dB NF
   - Test robustness with variations
