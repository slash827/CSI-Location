--- KEY INSIGHTS ---
• RSS measures total received power (path loss)
• SINR determines achievable data rate (signal quality)
• CQI provides discrete quality levels (0-15) for scheduling
• Metrics vary across subcarriers due to frequency selectivity
• All metrics degrade monotonically with distance
• CSIMetrics class automates industry-standard calculations

--- ML RELEVANCE ---
These metrics are essential features for ML-based positioning:

Features for Location Prediction:
  - Wideband RSS/SINR/CQI (overall signal quality)
  - Per-subcarrier statistics (mean, std, min, max)
  - CQI distribution (histogram, mode, range)
  - SINR dynamic range (max - min)
  - RSS vs SINR correlation
  - Per-RB CQI pattern

Why These Metrics Matter:
  1. RSS ∝ 1/distance² (path loss) → distance estimation
  2. SINR pattern varies with position → spatial fingerprint
  3. CQI distribution reveals multipath richness
  4. Frequency selectivity indicates scattering environment
